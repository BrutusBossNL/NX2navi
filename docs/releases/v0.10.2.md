## Fixed

- **Video memory no longer fills up while the camera moves** (by epicmeme546). The game lays its render targets out
  anew in every frame, one behind the other in one block of memory. One of them, a shadow map, changes its size with
  the view (1141x1141 to 1917x1917 in Kokiri Forest, a texel or two a frame), and every target behind it then lies at
  a new address in each frame. A moved target should take over the image of the one it replaces, but that image
  counted as in use for as long as the game had not recycled the old target's texture object, so every frame got about
  a dozen new images and each old one stayed for 120 frames or more: 6.7 GiB of them at 3416x1440 and 12.2 GiB at
  3840x2160, on top of the 10 GiB the area needs. A 20 GiB card ran full twice in five minutes of Kokiri Forest and a
  16 GiB card in Castle Town (images that could not be made or went to system memory, 2 to 4 frames a second, then
  the game ended). An image that another target was drawn over now goes to the next target of its size once nothing
  has drawn with it or sampled it for 16 frames, and past 80 % of the card's memory such images are freed at once
  instead of ten seconds later. Walking 28 metres from a save in Kokiri Forest and then around it at 3416x1440: 15.9 GiB
  of video memory in use before, 14 GiB again on each lap; 9.7 to 10.6 GiB now, with at most 2.2 GiB of released
  targets where there were 6.9 GiB, and 3,609 images made in the run where there were 6,970.
