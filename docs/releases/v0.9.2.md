## Fixed

- **A crash after switching FSR modes.** One FSR context served every mode, with another render size at each switch,
  and the first frame after a switch from Quality to Native AA could end the game inside AMD's driver. Each render size
  now gets a context of its own, as AMD's own sample makes them, and the one before goes a few frames later (a mode
  switch takes about 50 ms more); with dynamic resolution on, one context made for changing sizes takes every step.
- **The light line at the bottom or right edge with FSR or DLSS, still seen in some frames.** The scene's outermost
  rows and columns are now filled from the ones next to them in every frame the upscaler gets, not only after the
  scene moved up or to the left.
- **A blocky picture for a moment whenever the camera swung quickly with FSR or DLSS.** A fast swing of the camera
  around Link (11 to 18 degrees a frame) was taken for a cut to a new view, and the upscaler threw away what it had
  built up at every frame of the swing; at Ultra Performance that showed the scene at a third of the picture's size.
  Only a jump of the camera or a turn of 30 degrees or more in one frame counts as a cut now.

## Known issues

- At FSR's and DLSS's smaller modes, Link and other characters look coarser while they move: the upscaler gets only
  the camera's motion for them, not their own, so it can keep less of what it built up there.
- Over water, a bright line can still show at the very bottom of the picture now and then with FSR or DLSS: the water
  reads the scene before its last row is filled.
- Ultra Performance gains little frame rate over Quality with FSR 4. Most of a frame is the replay of the game's draws
  on the CPU, which does not shrink with the render size, and FSR 4 itself takes 3.1 ms of the GPU's time at Ultra
  Performance and 3.9 ms at Native AA on a Radeon RX 7900 XT (FSR 3.1: 0.5 ms). Measured standing in Hyrule Field at
  3416x1440: FSR 4 Native AA 52 fps, Quality 53, Ultra Performance 55; FSR 3.1 Native AA 55, Ultra Performance 66;
  without an upscaler 70.
